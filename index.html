Run your own little phone-case studio! Every customer walks in with a dream case in mind. Wipe it clean, spray on their colour, add their pattern, stick on their stickers — then hand it over and watch them smile.

How to play: 1) Tap the smudges to wipe • 2) Pick the colour, then drag across the case to paint • 3) Tap the pattern they asked for • 4) Pick a sticker, then tap the case to place it (tap a placed sticker to remove it) • 5) Tap Deliver! No timers, no losing — just craft.

20 customers, from easy first-timers to very picky regulars. Earn up to 3 stars per case.

How to Play

Wipe: tap every dusty smudge on the case until it sparkles.
Paint: choose the colour in the request card, then press and drag over the case until the paint meter is full.
Pattern: tap the pattern button that matches the request.
Stickers: tap a sticker in the tray, then tap the case where it should go. Tap a placed sticker to peel it off.
Deliver: when every step is ticked, tap the big Deliver button.
Wrong colour or pattern? No penalty to your score — just a gentle nudge and one fewer star. There is no way to lose.
